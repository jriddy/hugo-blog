## Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup water

## Instructions

1. Mix the flour and salt in a large bowl.
2. Add water gradually until a dough forms.
3. Knead for 5 minutes, then let rest.
4. Cook according to your preferred method.

## Part 2: Sauce

### Ingredients

- 1 cup tomatoes
- 2 cloves garlic

### Instructions

1. Simmer tomatoes and garlic for 10 minutes.
2. Serve over the prepared base.
